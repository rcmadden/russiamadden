About Chart Maker

What Is This?

The Red Track / Blue Track Chart Maker is a web-based tool designed to help Popmatics students visualize and understand musical concepts through interactive charts. The chart maker allows users to generate customizable charts that illustrate various musical elements, such as scales, and progressions.

Users can filter the charts by landscape, toggle scale degrees or note names on and off, and explore different musical keys. The interactive nature of the tool makes it easier for users to see patterns and relationships within scales, enhancing their learning experience. By using this tool, students can deepen their understanding of music and improve their understanding in a fun and engaging way.
